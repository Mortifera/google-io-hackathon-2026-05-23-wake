Why no one built this until now

Three things required, simultaneously.

01 · Intelligence per node

Frontier-class reasoning

Each entity's behavior has to be worth reading and believing if you knew the person.

02 · Output speed

4× faster, ticks forward on stage

A 200-node cascade resolves in seconds. You cannot demo a simulation that runs overnight.

03 · Cost at scale

$3.19 for 32 runs × 200 nodes

The Monte Carlo only works if the branches cost a few dollars, not thousands.

Frontier models had intelligence. Small models had speed. Neither had all three. Gemini 3.5 Flash is the first that does. The cost basis is the product.

Honest framing

Watch your decision before you make it.

Wake does not predict the future. It makes the consequence space of an action legible: the trajectories, the distribution, the pivotal variable.

World models are how embodied AI (robotics, self-driving) learns to reason about the physical world. Wake is that textual world model for decisions: one way to see any choice, any organization, and the impact it has on the world.
